# Art direction — The Lost & Found Office

**Status: proposed design direction, 2026-10-28.** Approved intent: editorial narrative, two rabbit holes, artifact-led interactions, blue plus two contrasting Kanji Signal-derived colours. Community artifact not yet selected.

## Identity
Postack Underflow is an experimental publication / research exhibition, not a SaaS dashboard. Opening: **We already solved this.** Structure: one observation → two problems → two rabbit holes → convergence → this article as a source-linked, indexable example. Restraint with wit; modern technical specimen rather than costume nostalgia.

These are **derived proposals**, not an assertion that the private Colors repo's study has already approved this combination.

### Kanji Signal discipline
Borrow *geometry and print logic*, not cultural decoration: hard-edged units, heavy type, sparse framed marks, English-readable labels, occasional narrow bands of related accent hues. A single mark has one dominant accent mass even when the site uses several accents. Do **not** add kanji merely for atmosphere; any Japanese text must carry accurate meaning and an English label, and be validated by a competent reader. No faux Japanese, rising sun, generic waves, neon gradients, or decorative CJK typography.

## Typography and layout
Inter/system sans for reading and application chrome. Strong scale contrast; monospaced technical metadata only. Hairline rules, annotation rails, crop marks, index numbers, square/precise frames. No eccentric display fonts, glassmorphism, generic rounded cards, or off-white AI-product clichés. The warm print ground is a deliberate material field, not default template beige.

## Artifact B — Community (alternatives, decision open)
1. **Switchboard** (preferred for trial): independently owned contributions connect through visible routes; user patches together problem-related threads, switches perspectives and sees disagreement. Risk: suggests a central operator controls speech; show connections as view-layer proposals, not canonical truth.
2. **Frequency Receiver**: tune into overlapping problem conversations across hosts; elegant narrative motion. Risk: metaphor obscures precise authorship/reply structure.
3. **Overlays**: translucent independent annotations align around one shared source; disagreements remain separate and inspectable. Risk: may become a static comparison tool rather than a community experience.

Choose through a small interaction sketch and actual reader comprehension test. Do not silently mark Switchboard accepted.

## Convergence
Both artifacts dissolve into shared source-linked records. The essay exposes its own problem, claims, evidence, provenance, index entry and portable conversation. No third fake machine.

## Scrollytelling
Vertical narrative with limited horizontal artifact exploration; sticky diagrams when helpful, natural mobile stacking, full content accessible without motion. Reduced-motion users get immediate state changes. Focus/hover reveal additional specifications, never required information.

## Source-to-interaction provenance
Follow `lumpesammlig`: encounter → situated reading → distinction → transformation → experiment → reflection. Follow `colors`: identity carrier = object + typography + framing; do not reduce design to hex tokens. Keep original research projects separate; document influence rather than copy implementations blindly.
