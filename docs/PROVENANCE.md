## New synthesis (hypotheses, not established implementations)
- **Two coupled deficits:** indexing and community.
- **Problem-oriented index:** offline structured extraction, cheap query-time retrieval.
- **The meta-essay:** the article describing the system is also its first indexed problem.
- **Portable discussion views:** one conversation rendered as blog thread, channel, and graph.
- **Four Words benchmark:** can a short query retrieve an existing solution without an agent browsing the web?

## Attribution practice
Link original source and exact version; label paraphrases; never fabricate CJ quotes, Stack Overflow performance claims, benchmark results, or ATProto compatibility. Keep a provenance trail for AI-assisted curation and distinguish suggestion from endorsement.
