2. **The lost shortcut:** what Google and Stack Overflow jointly supplied—indexing plus community editorial labour; no nostalgia or blanket anti-agent claim.
3. **The Index:** experience how source-linked descriptions, aliases, competing classifications and evidence determine retrieval. Show the frozen Four Words V1 including its negative audit.
4. **The Switchboard:** experience independent authored records and attributable, removable relationships. A connection is not consensus; a view is not the source.
5. **The Problem Desk:** one contribution challenges a classification, with a reversible preview of changed results and unchanged sources. A possibility, not a unified platform requirement.
6. **The meta ending:** this article itself has provenance, claims, revisions, open questions and source links; the public research history becomes part of the story.
